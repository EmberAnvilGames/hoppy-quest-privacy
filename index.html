Privacy Policy for Hoppy Quest

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Hoppy Quest is developed and published by Ember Anvil Games. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Hoppy Quest.

Information Collected

Hoppy Quest uses third-party services provided by Google that may automatically collect certain information.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Google Mobile Ads may collect and share information including:

  • Approximate location, which may be derived from your IP address
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Device or other identifiers, such as advertising identifiers

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

Google Play Games Services

Hoppy Quest may use Google Play Games Services for features such as leaderboards.

If you choose to use these features, Google Play Games may provide Hoppy Quest with a player identifier associated with your Play Games account. Google Play Games sign-in is optional and is not required to play the game.

Sharing of Information

Information described above may be processed or shared with Google through Google Mobile Ads and Google Play Games Services.

Ember Anvil Games does not sell personal information.

Data Security

Data transmitted by the Google services used by Hoppy Quest is encrypted in transit.

Accounts

Hoppy Quest does not create or maintain its own user accounts. Google Play Games accounts are created and managed by Google.

Data Retention and User Controls

Hoppy Quest does not maintain its own database of personal user profiles. Information collected by third-party Google services is retained and managed according to Google's applicable privacy policies and service terms.

Users may manage certain advertising, privacy, and Google account settings through their Android device and Google Account settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Hoppy Quest's features, third-party services, or data practices change. The latest version will be made available on this page.
